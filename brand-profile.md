## Identity
- **What it is (one sentence):** WoopSocial is a social media publishing platform that lets
  people — and their AI agents — generate, schedule, and publish content across nine social
  networks from one place.
- **Category:** Social media management / publishing infrastructure (agent-first).
- **Founded / location:** — (add if it becomes content-relevant)

## Positioning
- **What's different:** Built as publishing *rails for AI agents* — full public API, OpenAPI
  spec, and LLM index — while incumbents (Hootsuite, Sprout, Buffer) are dashboards built for
  humans clicking buttons. Backed by radically low per-profile pricing ($0.32–$0.95/profile/mo)
  and plans that scale to 2,500 accounts.
- **Why believe it:** The integration is public and working — the open-source
  [social-media-skills](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills) repo (106 MIT-licensed
  skills) routes agent publishing through WoopSocial today. Pricing is published and verifiable.
- **Point of view:** **"Social publishing is now an agent's job."** Humans set strategy, own
  the brand, and approve every post; agents do the production and publishing. Tools that make
  humans do the clicking are legacy.
- **Won't say:** "Fire your marketing team / fully autonomous social" (the human approves,
  always). No "set-and-forget autopilot" framing. Don't lean on "guaranteed lowest pricing in
  the world" in content unless the guarantee mechanism is stated alongside it.

## Audience
### Segment 1 — AI-agent builders & automators
- **Who:** Developers, technical founders, and automation-minded operators wiring AI agents
  (Claude Code, custom agents, workflow tools) to do real marketing work.
- **Cares about:** Agents that produce on-brand output instead of slop; a publishing API that
  just works; not paying enterprise-dashboard prices for what is essentially an API call.
- **Believes / objects:** Believes agents can do production work; skeptical of AI-marketing
  hype and of tools that bolt "AI" onto an old dashboard. Objection: "another scheduler?" —
  answer with the API/agent story, not feature lists.
- **Where:** LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky (our channels); they also live on X and GitHub.
- **Sophistication:** Expert on the tooling side; often intermediate on the marketing side —
  that's the gap the skills + rails fill.
- **Buyer vs follower note:** Practitioner and buyer are usually the same person (founder/dev
  who swipes the card). At agencies, the practitioner adopts free and champions upward.
- **Transformation:** Before — social is a manual chore or a generic-AI-slop pipeline they
  don't trust. After — their agent drafts on-brand, they approve, WoopSocial publishes; social
  runs like CI.

## Voice
- **Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 3/5 · Energy 3/5 · Humor 2/5 · Polish 3/5
- **We sound ___, never ___:**
  - Warm and plain-spoken, never dev-cool or aloof.
  - Transparent (numbers, decisions, misses), never salesy or hypey.
  - Confident about the agent-first thesis, never dismissive of human marketers.
  - Helpful-first (teach the workflow), never feature-dump-first.
- **Words we use:** "agent," "publishing rails," "skills," "human-approved," "on your explicit
  go," "connected accounts/profiles," "publish," "rails." Customers are "builders" or by role —
  never "users" in public copy.
- **Words we ban:** "game-changer," "revolutionize," "unleash," "supercharge," "10x your
  content," "AI-powered" as a standalone selling point, "content at scale" without substance,
  "🚀" strings, "Like if you agree," fake urgency ("only today!").
- **How we handle hard things:** Buffer-style transparency — own mistakes plainly and say what
  changed; announce pricing changes with the reasoning and the math; compare to competitors
  with verifiable, current facts only.
- **Signature devices:** Short declarative openers; show the actual workflow or command
  (e.g. real `npx` one-liners) rather than describing it; rare emoji (≤1, only when it adds
  warmth); **no hashtags**; plain punctuation.
- **Voice by platform:** LinkedIn — slightly fuller posts, still plain-spoken, no corporate
  drift. Threads/Bluesky — looser, conversational, build-in-public energy. Same voice,
  different sleeve.
- **Pronunciation guide:** WoopSocial = "WOOP-social" ("woop" as in "whoop").

## Operational defaults (tool skills read these)
- **AI & synthetic media policy:** **Proudly disclosed.** Content is agent-produced and
  human-approved, and we say so — it's the product demo. No AI avatars or voice clones without
  documented consent (own-voice-only if ever used). AI imagery allowed with disclosure where
  platforms require it. EU AI Act synthetic-media disclosure (from Aug 2026) is the floor;
  our stance already exceeds it.
- **Accessibility defaults:** Alt text on every image (plain, descriptive, no keyword
  stuffing); captions always-on for any video; plain language over jargon.
- **Visual identity:** → design-and-templates / brand kit (not yet defined — flag when a
  visual task arrives).

## Guardrails
- **Banned words/topics:** See voice bans. No engagement bait, no fabricated stats or
  metrics, no fake urgency or countdowns.
- **Compliance / legal:** "Guaranteed lowest per-profile pricing" only with the guarantee
  mechanism stated. Every cited number must be checkable at posting time.
- **Competitor rules:** Naming competitors is fine **when the comparison is factual,
  verifiable, and current** (pricing math, feature facts). Never mockery, never stale numbers.
- **Sensitivity:** Don't dunk on human marketers or "AI is replacing you" fear-framing — the
  thesis is humans direct, agents produce.

- **Loves:**
  1. *Build-in-public number post:* "This month the agent drafted 74 posts. I approved 61,
     rewrote 9, killed 4. Here's what the 4 got wrong →" — why: transparent, specific,
     demonstrates human-approval loop.
  2. *Workflow demo:* "My Claude Code agent planned next week's content and queued it to
     3 platforms. One command: `npx skills add …`" — why: shows the actual command, CTA is
     the free repo.
  3. *Factual comparison:* "Managing 100 profiles: Sprout ~$X/profile, WoopSocial $0.49.
     Here's the math + what you give up" — why: verifiable numbers, honest about trade-offs.
  4. *Teaching post:* "Your agent writes slop because it has no brand context. Fix: a
     brand-profile file every skill reads first. Here's the template" — why: helpful-first,
     sells the thesis without pitching.
- **Hates:**
  1. "🚀 AI is a GAME-CHANGER for social media! Here are 10 tools you NEED in 2026 🧵👇" —
     why: hype lexicon, listicle bait, zero lived experience.
  2. "Marketing teams are obsolete. Let AI run your social on autopilot." — why: violates the
     human-approval spine and dunks on the audience's colleagues.
